**Brief Connect 2.9 will expand the platform beyond briefing into the coordination and administration of official meetings.**

We will introduce support for managing official meetings from committee level through to Cabinet. Teams will be able to link records and documents to meetings, construct agendas, and invite executives to digitally explore board papers within Brief Connect.

**What this means for customers:** less manual handling and fewer disconnected tools across the full lifecycle, from drafting advice through to meeting coordination.
